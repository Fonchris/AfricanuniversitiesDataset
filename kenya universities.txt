# 11. Why we should NOT simply merge CUE + TVETA

This is extremely important for your database.

A university can also have a TVET institute.

For example, TVETA's current database contains:

* **Kabarak University TVET Institute**
* **Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology TVET Directorate**
* **Kibabii University Directorate of TVET**
* **Tharaka University TVET Directorate**
* **Turkana University College TVET Institute**
* **St Paul's University TVET Institute**
* **Marist International University College**

These are visible in TVETA's programme/institution records. ([TVET Authority][8])

Therefore, if we simply combine CUE and TVETA records, we'd create duplicates.

### The correct approach is:

```text
Institution
    ↓
University / TVET / College / Institute
    ↓
Campus / Directorate / Faculty
```

For example:

```text
Kabarak University
    └── Kabarak University TVET Institute
```

rather than treating them as two unrelated universities.

---

# 12. Accreditation/status needs to be preserved

Another major reason Kenya requires a structured dataset is that **accreditation status is not necessarily permanent**.

TVETA's current register explicitly distinguishes:

* `Registered and Licensed`
* `Registered Only`
* `Expired License`
* `Deregistered`

For example, the current TVETA records show institutions with expired licences alongside currently licensed institutions. ([TVET Authority][6])

CUE similarly distinguishes chartered universities, constituent colleges and institutions with Letters of Interim Authority. ([Cue][3])

So I would **never reduce the data to simply `accredited: true/false`**.
